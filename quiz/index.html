Before you start

  • Arrays are numbered from 1.
  • The value 0 means empty for TOP and REAR, and null for NEXT.
  • An empty queue has FRONT = 1 and REAR = 0. In this lesson’s model, FRONT stays on cell 1 while items are only added.
  • This practice covers adding only: push, enqueue, and one linked-list insert.
  • There are 10 questions. Choose one option for each. Answers are hidden until you submit.
